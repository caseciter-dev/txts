          UPON hearing the counsel the Court made the following
                             O R D E R
1. This Suo Moto Writ Petition has been registered in pursuance  
of the directions in judgement dated 12.03.2026 passed by this  
Court in WP (C) No. 13029/1985. In that Writ Petition, this Court  
was occasioned to deal with various issues concerning the degrading  
air quality in the National Capital Region (NCR). Vide the above-
cited judgment, we recognized that the scope of the proceedings in  
the said Writ Petition were multi-faceted and required a more  
contemporary method of addressing the wide variety of concerns  
raised therein, thus directing the registration of five separate  
Suo Moto Writ Petitions, each pertaining to a separate aspect of  
air pollution in NCR. It was also clarified that these newly  
registered cases would not be the closure of the proceedings in the  
older Writ Petition, but amount to a re-shaping of the same.
2. Ms. Aishwarya Bhati, learned ASG on behalf of the Union of  
India and the Commission for Air Quality Management in National  
Capital Region and Adjoining Areas (CAQM), Ms. Aparajita Singh, Ms.  
Uttara Babbar, and Ms. Shibani Ghose, learned  Amici Curiae , and 
other Senior Counsel/Counsel for the various parties have, thus,  
addressed us on the steps taken meanwhile towards curbing the air  
pollution in Delhi-NCR.
3. Ms. Bhati has highlighted some of the efforts taken by the  

--- Page 4 ---
3
Union of India as well as the different States/NCT, including,  
inter alia, the restrictions on vehicular emissions and measures to 
reduce stubble burning. At the same time, learned Amici Curiae have 
pointed out that the compliance of previous orders, especially  
orders dated 21.01.2026 and 23.02.2026, is yet to be properly  
reported to this Court.
4. Having  heard  learned  Amici  Curiae ,  ASG,  and  senior  
counsel/counsel for the parties, it seems to us that substantive  
consideration by this Court on each of the issues would require  
that all the States place their submissions on (i) the steps taken  
by them; (ii) their responses to each of the suggestions of the  
CAQM and the Amici; and (iii) any action that they independently  
propose to take moving forward. We thus grant the States two weeks’  
time to place on record their submissions.
5. During the course of proceedings, we were also apprised by the  
learned  Amici Curiae , who placed before this Bench a graphical  
representation depicting the status of compliance with the Graded  
Response Action Plan (GRAP) for the period between 21.10.2026 and  
03.11.2026. The data so presented indicates a substantial decline  
in incidents of stubble burning across Delhi and the States of  
Uttar Pradesh, Haryana and Rajasthan. While this development is  
certainly laudable, the same data also brings to the fore a  
converse and equally pressing concern, namely, the sharp increase  
in issues attributable to vehicular emissions.
6. It must be borne in mind that Delhi and the National Capital  
Region  (NCR),  by  virtue  of  their  geographical  and  economic  
interconnectedness, bear the cumulative burden of vehicular traffic  
originating not merely within their own boundaries but also from  
the neighbouring States of Uttar Pradesh, Haryana and Rajasthan.  
The situation is further compounded by the substantial volume of  
both commercial and non-commercial vehicles entering and plying  
within the city from different parts of the region, thereby adding  
to the existing vehicular emission load.

--- Page 5 ---
4
7. There can be little doubt that the issue warrants urgent and  
immediate  intervention.  Accordingly,  we  direct  the  Municipal  
Corporation of Delhi (MCD), the Government of NCT of Delhi (GNCTD),  
the Delhi Police (Traffic Department), as well as the States of  
Haryana, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh, to file their respective  
detailed responses before the next date of hearing, specifically  
addressing the following aspects:
(i) The existing regulatory framework and enforcement mechanisms  
governing the entry and plying of commercial and non-commercial  
vehicles within Delhi and the National Capital Region, particularly  
those originating from neighbouring States;
(ii) The measures presently in place to identify, monitor and  
regulate vehicles that do not conform to the prescribed emission  
standards, including the enforcement of Pollution Under Control  
(PUC) certification requirements;
(iii) The steps taken to prevent the entry and operation of over-
age, end-of-life, or otherwise non-compliant vehicles within the  
National Capital Region;
(iv)  The  enforcement  measures  undertaken  against  violations,  
including the number of vehicles inspected, challaned, denied entry  
or otherwise proceeded against during the relevant period;
(v) The coordination mechanisms between the authorities of Delhi  
and the neighbouring States for regulating inter-State vehicular  
movement and ensuring compliance with applicable emission norms;  
and
(vi) Any further short-term and long-term measures proposed or  
contemplated to mitigate vehicular emissions and reduce their  
contribution to the deteriorating air quality in Delhi and the  
National Capital Region.
The  respective  authorities  shall  also  indicate  the  specific  
measures  proposed  to  strengthen  the  existing  enforcement

5
mechanisms,  together  with  a  time-bound  roadmap  for  their  
implementation.
8. In the interest of giving adequate and full attention to each  
facet of the larger matter, we direct the Registry to list the  
following cases on the date indicated:
(i) SMW  (C)  No.12/2026  (Vehicular  Emissions  and  Pollution)-  
23.11.2026.
(ii) SMW (C) No.13/2026 (Conservation and Enhancement of Green  
Cover)- 30.11.2026.
(iii) SMW (C) No.14/2026 (Pollution by Construction Activities,  
Power Plants, and Other Industries)- 26.11.2026.
(iv) SMW (C) No.15/2026 (Solid Waste Management and Burning of Crop  
Residue and Firecrackers)- 25.11.2026.
9. The learned  Amici are requested to file their short notes,  
apart from any lengthier written submissions, at least two days  
prior to the date of hearing of the respective issue(s) so as to  
enable the parties to address this Court on the various concerns  
that are raised therein.
10. The Registry shall ensure that Paragraph 19 of the judgment  
dated 12.03.2026 is duly complied with and the instant Suo Moto  
Writ Petition shall be treated as the lead matter, to be listed on  
each of the above dates.
11. In the course of hearing, the learned  Amici Curiae  have  
requested that Ms. Shibani Ghose be recorded as the ‘filing  
Amicus’, so that she is able to file the relevant documents through  
the e-filing portal. The Registry is directed to do the needful and  
allow the learned  Amici to e-file their documents through an  
appropriate mechanism.
