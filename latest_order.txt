IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA 
CRIMINAL ORIGINAL JURISDICTION 
O R D E R 
1. This transfer petition seeks the transfer of Sessions 
Case No.737/2026 (arising out of FIR No.RC21720233A0005), 
titled “CBI v. Narayan Shukla & Anr.”, from the Court of 
Special Judge, Anti-Corruption, CBI, West Lucknow, U.P. to 
the Court of Special Judge (PC Act) CBI, Rouse Avenue Court, 
Central District, New Delhi. 
2. Heard learned counsel for the parties and carefully 
perused the material placed on record. 
3. It appears that the Petitioners-accused persons are 
facing trial in FIR bearing RC No. 217-2019-A-0009 before 
the Rouse Avenue Courts, New Delhi. However, the subject 
trial, emerging from FIR bearing RC No. 217-2023-A-0005, is 
presently ongoing in Lucknow, U.P. 
4. Learned counsel appearing for the Petitioners submits 
TRANSFER PETITION (CRL.) NO.683/2026 
S.N. SHUKLA & ANR. PETITIONER(S) 
                              VERSUS
CENTRAL BUREAU OF INVESTIGATION RESPONDENT(S)
1

--- Page 3 ---
that both cases emerge from a connected set of facts and the 
allegations substantially overlap, even though the offences 
alleged may be different. The learned Additional Solicitor 
General, representing the CBI, fairly submits that there 
would be no difficulty if both the trials are conducted by a 
Court of competent jurisdiction in New Delhi. 
5. The Transfer Petition is, thus, allowed, subject to the 
following conditions: 
(i) The Petitioners shall fully cooperate with the 
ongoing trial and shall not seek any unnecessary 
adjournment; and 
(ii) The prosecution/CBI may, in its wisdom, identify 
such official witnesses who can be conveniently and 
effectively examined through online linkages/video 
conferencing. The Transferee Court shall permit them to 
be examined online, in accordance with applicable Rules 
and procedures. 
6. Accordingly, Sessions Case No.737/2026 titled “CBI v. 
Narayan Shukla & Anr.”, which is pending before the Court of 
Special Judge, Anti-Corruption, CBI, West Lucknow, U.P. is 
ordered to be transferred to the Court of Special Judge (PC 
Act) CBI, Rouse Avenue Court, Central District, New Delhi.  
7. The records be forwarded to the Transferee Court 
forthwith.

JASPER COLIN RESEARCH INC. PETITIONER(S)
VERSUS
KELLTON TECH SOLUTIONS 
LIMITED
RESPONDENT(S)
O R D E R
1. This petition under Section 11(6) of the Arbitration and  
Conciliation Act, 1996 (Act), seeks the appointment of a sole  
arbitrator  to  adjudicate  disputes  arising  from  a  Software  
Development  Agreement  executed  between  the  Petitioner  and  the 
Respondent.
2. Briefly observed, the Petitioner, Jasper Colin Research Inc.,  
is a company incorporated in the United States of America, engaged  
in  the  business  of  research/survey  and  allied  services.  The  
Respondent, Kellton Tech Solutions Limited, is a software company  
incorporated  in  India.  The  parties  entered  into  a  Software  
Development  Agreement  dated  24.02.2025  (SDA),  whereunder  the  
Respondent was engaged as to design, develop, test, deploy, and  
implement a custom Survey Module for the Petitioner. The work was  
to proceed in defined milestones, with a stipulated contractual  
target  date  for  delivery  of  the  software.  Clause  19  thereof 
provides that disputes not resolved through amicable negotiation  
within 30 days shall be referred to a Sole Arbitrator under the  
Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with the seat at New Delhi.

--- Page 7 ---
2
3. It is alleged that the Respondent delayed delivery at various  
stages, delivered defective and incomplete work, and failed to  
complete milestones 3 and 4 and the ‘Code Knowledge Transfer’  
within the Target Date passing without delivery. The Respondent  
has,  per contra , disputed these allegations and attributed the  
delays  the  Petitioner’s  actions/inactions.  The  Petitioner,  
thereafter,  terminated  the  Agreement  by  communication  dated  
29.07.2026,  while  the  Respondent  has  issued  communications  
demanding payment.
4. The  Petitioner,  thus,  issued  an  arbitration  notice  dated 
21.08.2026 to the Respondent, invoking Clause 19 and suggesting the  
name of a former Judge of this Court for appointment as the Sole  
Arbitrator. It is stated that the Respondent did not reply within  
15 days, prompting the Petitioner to file the instant Petition  
under Section 11 of the Act.
5. We have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the  
material placed on record.
6. Keeping in view the facts and circumstances recorded above and  
the relevant provision(s) under the SDA, we deem it just and proper  
to allow the instant Arbitration Petition and appoint Justice Rekha 
Palli, former Judge, Delhi High Court  as the Sole Arbitrator to  
resolve the disputes between the parties. The seat and venue of  
arbitration, in accordance with the arbitration agreement, shall be  
New Delhi, India.

3
7. The  Arbitrator  shall  have  the  liberty  to  fix  her  own  
remuneration/fees. Ordered accordingly.
8. Pending  interlocutory  applications,  if  any,  also  stand  
disposed of.

obtain consent of the respondents for the sale of the  
stock, shall remain stayed. However, the appellant shall 
abide by its undertaking to the effect that it will not 
sell its stock to the related parties, subject to  
further compliance by the appellant of the order dated  
14.06.2024 passed by the National Company Law Tribunal,  
Jaipur Bench in C.P No.14/241-242/JPR/2024.”

we dispose of the instant appeal in the following terms:

is made absolute and shall remain in force during pendency of  
the appeal.
