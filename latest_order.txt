          UPON hearing the counsel the Court made the following
                             O R D E R
1. We have heard learned senior counsel/counsel for the parties  
at a considerable length on the issue of the nature and composition  
of the Committee that may be entrusted with the responsibility of  
completing all the projects of the respondent-builder(s) or their  
sister concerns and allied and associated companies.
2. Till appropriate orders are passed on this issue, however, we  
deem it appropriate to modify our order dated 13.07.2026 with  
respect to freezing of the accounts of the respondent-builder(s) to  
the following limited extent:
(i) The Resolution Professional (RP) is permitted to pay the  
electricity  charges  to  prevent  disconnection  of  the  
electricity supply;
(ii) The RP may also release a part of the dues payable to the  
employees of the company out of the funds to be provided by  
the lending financial institutions;
(iii)Wherever the projects are complete and the apartments  
have been handed over, the private and official Respondents  
are directed to execute and register sale deeds in favour of  
the allotees after verifying their allotments. However, this  
does not permit the respondents to create third party rights,  
i.e., the sale deeds/conveyance deeds shall be executed only  
in favour of previous allotees, if the project is complete and  
an occupancy certificate has been issued;

(iv) The RP may also release other payments with respect to  
basic amenities, such as water supply, etc.; and
(v) The  lending  financial  institutions  are  directed  to  
provide adequate funds to the RP for the above purposes.
3. Paragraph 4 of the order dated 09.10.2026 is also modified and  
clarified to the effect that payment of compensation as per the  
Awards passed by RERA to senior citizens aged 75 and above be  
considered and expedited.
4. List these cases for further hearing on 09.12.2026.
5. The Registry is directed to de-tag and separately list all  
those matters which do not pertain to Parsvnath Developers and  
their  associated  companies  on  10.12.2026.  Meanwhile,  the  
respondent-builder(s) in such other cases are directed to release  
the payments as per the orders passed against them by RERA.
