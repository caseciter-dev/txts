          UPON hearing the counsel the Court made the following
                             O R D E R
1. The present batch of cases raises concerns regarding the
selective use of video clips of court proceedings for commercial
gain, along with certain ancillary issues arising therefrom.
2. Vide an order dated 24.07.2026, this Court issued notice and
directed the Union of India to put up a proposal with respect to
the prayers made in the Writ Petition. Vide paragraphs 9 and 11 of
that order, the following interim directions were also issued:
“9. As an interim measure, it is directed that there
shall be no extraction, dissemination, monetisation,
posting,  re-posting,  uploading,  transmitting,
modification, storing, or hosting of the audio-video
recording(s) of the judicial proceedings on social media
5

--- Page 7 ---
or any other digital platforms without prior permission
of the Secretary General of the Supreme Court of India
or the Registrar Generals of the jurisdictional High
Courts.
xxx xxx xxx
11. However, it is clarified that this order shall have
no bearing on the reporting of Court proceedings by
recognized news outlets.”
3. These interim directions were reiterated on 09.10.2026 in the
following terms:
“5. Interim directions issued on 24.07.2026 prohibiting
extraction, dissemination, monetisation, posting, re-
posting, uploading, transmitting, modification, storing,
or  hosting  of  the  audio-video  recording(s)  of  the
judicial  proceedings  on  social  media  or  any  other
digital  platforms  without  prior  permission  of  the
Secretary General of the Supreme Court of India or the
Registrar Generals of the jurisdictional High Courts
shall  continue  to  operate  and  shall  be  faithfully
complied with by all social media channels.”
4. In deference to the previous orders, various High Courts have
filed their status reports. The learned Solicitor General of India
submits that the suggestions and recommendations made by the High
Courts, as well as the observations made by this Court in its
interim orders from time to time, are presently under consideration
of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY),
which is also examining the formulation of an appropriate policy in
this regard. While formulating the policy framework, MeitY shall
ensure strict compliance with the interim directions contained in
paragraph 9 of the order dated 24.07.2026, as clarified by the
subsequent order dated 09.10.2026. MeitY shall also put in place
6

--- Page 8 ---
appropriate measures to take action against those found to have
violated the said directions.
5. Post the matters on 25.11.2026.
